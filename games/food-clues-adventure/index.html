Vocabulary Bank: Food & Lifestyle

Review the definitions, key context hints, and collocations found across your 30 mission questions.

Teacher Tip: When reading a text, look out for contextual indicators such as where things grow (under the ground), what color they are (green leaves, yellow fat), or what type of container holds them (a glass jar, a tin can).

1. Meats & Alternatives

Beef: Meat from a cow.
Lamb: Meat from a young sheep.
Chicken: Highly popular bird meat.
Vegetarian: A person who chooses never to eat meat or fish.

2. Key Vegetables

Broccoli: A healthy green vegetable looking like small trees.
Onions: Round bulbs making you cry when chopped.
Garlic: Small, white cloves with a sharp, delicious smell.
Lettuce: Soft green leaves used as a salad base.

3. Sweet Fruits

Banana: Long fruit with a yellow protective skin.
Grapes: Tiny round fruits growing together in bunches.
Pear: Narrow at the top, wide and juicy at the bottom.

4. Packing & Containers

Jar: A solid glass container with a lid for jam or honey.
Carton: A clean cardboard box for milk or orange juice.
Packet: A paper or plastic wrap for dry goods like biscuits or rice.

5. Eating Habits

Takeaway: Warm restaurant meals brought home instead of eaten inside.
Snack: Small portions of food eaten in between main daily meals.
Healthy: Foods like fruits keeping the human body clean and strong.

6. Kitchenware & Appliances

Fridge: A cool kitchen appliance keeping dairy and items fresh.
Bowl: A deep circular dish perfect for morning cereal or warm soups.
