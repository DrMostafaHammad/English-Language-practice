Travel Vocabulary Bank

Use this review page before or after the game. The words below are practical travel words for airports, stations, hotels, and visiting a city.

Easy study tip: do not learn words alone. Learn a word with a short meaning and one simple example, such as gate -> the place where you wait before you get on a plane.
Passport official travel document
Reservation a room you booked before arrival
Souvenir something you buy to remember a trip

Passport

An official document that shows who you are when you travel to another country.

  • Example: I showed my passport at the airport.
  • Useful phrase: passport control

Boarding pass

The paper or phone ticket you show before you get on the plane.

  • Example: Please keep your boarding pass in your hand.
  • Useful phrase: show your boarding pass

Suitcase

A large bag for clothes and travel items.

  • Example: Her suitcase is too heavy.
  • Useful phrase: pack a suitcase

Gate

The place in an airport where people wait before they board the plane.

  • Example: We are waiting at gate 12.
  • Useful phrase: go to the gate

Platform

The place in a station where you wait for a train.

  • Example: Our train leaves from platform 4.
  • Useful phrase: on the platform

Reservation

An arranged booking for a room, seat, or table.

  • Example: I have a reservation for two nights.
  • Useful phrase: make a reservation

Receptionist

The person at the front desk in a hotel.

  • Example: The receptionist gave us the key.
  • Useful phrase: ask the receptionist

Return ticket

A ticket for going somewhere and then coming back.

  • Example: We bought a return ticket to Ankara.
  • Useful phrase: buy a return ticket

Sightseeing

Visiting famous or interesting places in a city.

  • Example: We did some sightseeing after lunch.
  • Useful phrase: go sightseeing

Souvenir

Something you buy or keep to remember a place you visited.

  • Example: Mia bought a souvenir for her sister.
  • Useful phrase: souvenir shop
Travel words are easier to remember in short scenes: airport, hotel, station, and city centre. Try to imagine where the word is used each time.
